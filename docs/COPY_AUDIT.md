# Copy audit — generic financial-services language

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Scope:** Visitor-facing `src` copy (pages, services, SEO meta, CTAs, resources)  
**Brand anchors (verified):** More Than Numbers · Clarity · Trust · Confidence · Organized Books → Smarter Decisions → Stronger Business  
**Differentiation to favor:** understandable information · organized processes · human communication · client clarity · founder-led service · Calgary/local relevance  
**Rule:** No unsupported claims (CPA, testimonials, stats, guarantees).

---

## Search terms checked

Including (case-insensitive): trusted partner, tailored solutions, comprehensive services, excellence, premier, one-stop shop, dedicated professionals, your success is our success, personalized solutions — plus close cousins (tailored, personalized, client-centered, trusted sounding board, trusted references, clarity and care).

**Not found on the site (good):** trusted partner, comprehensive services, excellence, premier, one-stop shop, dedicated professionals, your success is our success, personalized solutions (as a pitch).

---

## Residual watchlist (acceptable / monitor)

- Heavy use of “support” and “clarity” is intentional brand vocabulary; avoid stacking empty modifiers in front of them in future edits.
- Service `audiences` lists remain soft self-ID cues—not eligibility requirements.
- Do not reintroduce “tailored solutions,” “trusted partner,” or “comprehensive packages” without concrete scope behind them.

---

## Brand differentiation checklist (post-audit)

Visitor-facing copy now leans on:

1. **Understandable financial information** — plain language, numbers you can use  
2. **Organized processes** — books, payroll records, tax materials in order  
3. **Human communication** — listening, updates, room for questions  
4. **Client clarity** — decide with a clearer view of where things stand  
5. **Founder-led service** — Sari / founder-led framing  
6. **Calgary / local** — location named without invented neighbourhood claims  

No new credentials, guarantees, or social proof were added.
